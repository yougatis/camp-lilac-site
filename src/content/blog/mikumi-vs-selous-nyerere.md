If you are in Dar es Salaam or on Zanzibar and want a safari without crossing the country to the Serengeti, the choice comes down to two parks: Mikumi National Park, on the highway and the railway west of the city, and Nyerere National Park, the northern part of the old Selous Game Reserve, to the south. We run safaris in Mikumi, so you know where we stand — but we have sent plenty of guests to Nyerere when it was the better fit, and this is the comparison we give them.

## The two parks in one table

| | Mikumi National Park | Nyerere National Park (Selous) |
|---|---|---|
| From Dar es Salaam | 3.5 h by SGR train + transfer, or 5–6 h by road | 45-minute flight, or 6–8 h on rough roads |
| From Zanzibar | Ferry or 20-min flight to Dar, then as above | Direct light-aircraft flights, or via Dar |
| Park fee, non-resident adult | US$35.40 per 24 h (US$30 + VAT) | US$82.60 per 24 h (US$70 + VAT) |
| Size | 3,230 km² | About 30,000 km² — one of Africa’s largest parks |
| Landscape | Open Mkata floodplain with baobabs and acacias; miombo woodland in the south | Rufiji River, lakes and channels, palm forest, woodland |
| Signature wildlife | Elephant, lion (sometimes in trees), giraffe, zebra, buffalo, wildebeest, eland, hippo, 400+ birds | Elephant, lion, hippo, crocodile, wild dog, sable, giraffe (northern sector), huge bird lists |
| Activities | Game drives in a private open 4×4; night drives only from inside-park lodges | Game drives, boat safaris on the Rufiji, walking safaris, fly-camping |
| Typical trip | 1–3 days | 3–4 days |
| Budget for two days, per person | About US$440 from Dar by train, all-in | Several times that, because of flights and fees |
| Best months | June–October; green and quiet November–February; open all year | June–October; some camps close in the long rains (April–May) |
| Children | Easy: short drives, open country, no flights | Older children do well; boat safaris are a highlight |

## Where Mikumi wins

**Time.** Mikumi is the only Tanzanian big-game park on a railway line. Leave Dar es Salaam after breakfast and you are on a game drive the same afternoon; a real two-day safari fits a weekend. Nyerere needs a flight each way or most of a day on the road, so a two-day trip is mostly travel.

**Cost.** Park fees are less than half of Nyerere’s, there are no flights to pay for, and accommodation outside the park (where Camp Lilac is) carries no concession fees. A weekend in Mikumi for two people costs roughly what one person pays to fly into Nyerere.

**Easy wildlife.** The Mkata floodplain is open grassland. Animals are visible from a long way off, which matters when you have one afternoon and one morning, and it is why Mikumi is so good for first-time safari-goers and families with young children.

**Flexibility.** Arrive at any hour by train, add a night in the Udzungwa Mountains for the Sanje Waterfall, or stop over on the way south to Ruaha. Nyerere is a destination you commit to.

## Where Nyerere wins

**Wilderness and scale.** Nyerere is ten times the size of Mikumi, with far fewer vehicles per sighting. If you want to feel a long way from anywhere, this is the park.

**Boat and walking safaris.** The Rufiji River is the reason to go: boat safaris past hippos, crocodiles and elephants coming down to drink, plus walking safaris with armed rangers and fly-camping. Mikumi is a vehicle park.

**Wild dogs.** Nyerere has one of Africa’s most important populations of African wild dog. Mikumi has them too, in the southern woodland, but sightings are a matter of luck.

**Exclusivity.** The best Nyerere camps are small, remote and all-inclusive, and they are priced accordingly. If budget is not a constraint and you have three or four days, it is a superb park.

## Our honest verdict, by traveller

- **A weekend from Dar es Salaam, or a 2–3 day break from Zanzibar:** Mikumi, without question. Nyerere cannot be done well in that time. See [safari from Dar es Salaam](/safari-from-dar-es-salaam) and [safari from Zanzibar](/safari-from-zanzibar).
- **A first safari, or a family with children under ten:** Mikumi. Short drives, lots of animals, no small planes, half the fees, and tents with air conditioning at the end of the day.
- **Budget-conscious travellers:** Mikumi — see [what a Mikumi safari costs](/blog/mikumi-safari-cost).
- **Four days or more, flights in the budget, and you want river and walking safaris:** Nyerere. Or, better still, both: Mikumi first by train, then fly into Nyerere from Dar es Salaam, and you will have seen the two faces of the Southern Circuit.
- **Photographers:** it depends on the subject. Open-plain herds, lions in trees and the Mikumi mountain ridge as a backdrop — Mikumi. Water, light on the river and wild dogs — Nyerere.

## Can you combine them?

Yes, and it is a good trip: two or three days in Mikumi reached by train, back to Dar es Salaam, then a flight into Nyerere for three nights.
